Our Project
Project Scope

One package, raw woodwork to final finishing

A complete project typically covers the following stages. You can include all of them or only the ones you need.

  1. Project Planning & MeasurementsSite visit, room-by-room measurement and a woodwork schedule aligned with your construction timeline.
  2. Woodwork Design & SelectionDoor, window, wardrobe and kitchen designs; wood type, veneer and finish samples approved by you.
  3. Door & Window ManufacturingAll doors, frames, windows and shutters produced together in our workshop for consistent profiles and sizes.
  4. Bedroom & Wardrobe ManufacturingBeds, fitted wardrobes, dressing and side tables for every bedroom, matched in design and finish.
  5. Kitchen WoodworkCabinets, pantry and island units built for daily use and finished in hard-wearing PU.
  6. Interior Wooden DecorationWall panels, ceiling features, staircases, railings and other decorative or structural woodwork.
  7. Wood Polishing & FinishingEvery surface stained and finished by our polishing team — the step we specialise in.
  8. Final Installation & CompletionInstallation, hardware fitting, snagging and a walkthrough with you before handover.

Why One Team

Consistency you can only get from a single contractor

When different people make the doors, the wardrobes and the polish, the house ends up with three shades of "walnut". We avoid that by controlling every step.

  • One wood, one stain, one finish throughout the home
  • One schedule aligned with your builder and electrician
  • One quotation, one point of contact, one responsibility
  • Phased work possible — floor by floor or room by room
