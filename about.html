Craftsmanship & Quality

Our commitment to quality

Good furniture should look beautiful on day one and still feel solid years later. These are the standards we hold ourselves to on every job.

Right wood for the job

We explain the differences between wood types, boards and veneers honestly, and recommend what suits your use, climate and budget — not what's easiest for us.

Solid construction

Proper joinery, quality hardware and careful assembly — so drawers glide, doors close true and frames stay square through daily family use.

Finish that lasts

Surfaces are properly prepared, sanded and sealed before polishing. The result is an even, smooth finish that resists everyday wear and is easy to maintain.

Accurate measurements

We measure your space ourselves wherever possible, and double-check before cutting — fitted furniture only looks right when it fits exactly.

Clear, written quotations

You receive an itemised quotation before work begins. If anything changes along the way, we discuss and agree it with you first.

Final inspection with you

Nothing is finished until you've seen it, opened every door and drawer, and are happy with the result.

How We Work

Our working process

  1. Consultation. You tell us what you need — on WhatsApp, by phone or through the website. We ask the right questions about size, style, wood and budget.
  2. Site visit & measurement. For fitted or large pieces we visit your home or site to measure and look at the space.
  3. Design & quotation. We confirm the design, suggest finishes and send a clear, itemised quotation with a realistic timeline.
  4. Manufacturing. Your furniture is built in our workshop. You're welcome to visit and see the progress.
  5. Polishing & finishing. Pieces are sanded, sealed and polished in the finish you chose.
  6. Delivery & installation. We deliver, install, clean up and walk through everything with you.
