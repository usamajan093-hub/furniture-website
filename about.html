About AM Royal Interiors

Complete Polish Work, Deco & All Kinds of Woodwork — for Homes, Factories & Buildings.

Who We Are

Polish contractors who build, too

AM Royal Interiors are contractors for complete polish work in homes, factories and buildings, based in Manzoor Colony and serving Karachi & surrounding areas.

Polish is our specialty: furniture polish, bedroom sets, chairs and tables, doors and windows, full buildings and factory contracts — in PU, melamine, lacquer, matt or glossy. But we are not only polishers. We manufacture doors, windows, bedroom sets, wardrobes, dressing tables, chairs, tables and deco in our own workshop, so you can order the piece, the room or the whole building from one contractor.

Craftsmanship & Quality

What we hold ourselves to

Polish is never an afterthought

Surfaces are sanded in stages, filled, sealed, stained to an approved sample and coated thinly, several times. That's why our finishes look even and last.

Contract discipline

Factory and building work means quantity, deadlines and one standard on every piece. We plan the team around your schedule.

Built to last

Proper joinery, quality hardware and careful installation — so doors close true and drawers glide for years.

Measured on site

We measure ourselves and double-check before cutting. Fitted woodwork only looks right when it fits exactly.

Clear, written quotations

Itemised before work begins. If something changes, we discuss and agree it with you first.

Inspected with you

Nothing is finished until you've seen it in daylight and under room lighting and are happy.

How We Work

Our working process

  1. Consultation. Tell us the scope — polish only, specific pieces, a factory contract or a complete building.
  2. Site visit & measurement. We measure and look at the space, the light and the existing woodwork.
  3. Design, samples & quotation. Designs, wood and finish samples on your actual wood, and a clear itemised quote.
  4. Manufacturing. Doors, windows, furniture, deco and fitted woodwork built in our workshop.
  5. Installation. Fitted on site, hardware adjusted, snagging done.
  6. Polish & finishing. Every surface stained and polished by our polish team, then inspected with you.
